This is a project I started really recently! I know a lot of us spend too much time on our smartphones, so for a while I've been interested in moving to a simpler phone- I really love flip phones and when I was younger my first phone was a flip phone, then a flip phone with a keyboard which I've always missed. The main thing stopping me is that so much of our day is connected with a smartphone- maps, email, messaging, and having the internet immediatly available to you. I found out recently that there is a big modding community for dumb phones!

I'm currently modding it for: voice to text, spotify, gmail, maps, and more when I think of it and fully transition from my iphone.
