I'm working with a team of six to make a game that is similar to webfishing- a relaxing multiplayer game for friends to play in the background of hanging out. You take pictures of random birds and sell photos of them to upgrade your camera and make money. We made this game for a game production class at UF, however we're still planning on working on it! We're exciting to keep working on it and hopefully eventually release it on steam.

Check out our behance and Itch.io page! We're still a work in progress but soon we should have an early build to play online!
